# Executive Summary

## Campaign readout

This simulated six-week campaign sample generated 35,520 clicks, 2,517 conversions, EUR 212,063 revenue and 5.62 blended ROAS.

## Main observations

- Email has the strongest ROAS at 27.39, driven by high-intent traffic and low CPC.
- Paid Search contributes the highest conversion volume with 966 conversions.
- Display has the lowest ROAS at 2.52; it should be reviewed for audience quality, landing page speed and budget allocation.

| Email | 7,203 | 756 | 10.5% | EUR 2,349 | 27.39 |
| Paid Search | 12,070 | 966 | 8.0% | EUR 15,399 | 5.64 |
| Paid Social | 10,179 | 508 | 5.0% | EUR 12,006 | 3.39 |

## Practical recommendations

- Protect budget for high-intent Search and retention Email activity while monitoring saturation.
- Improve Display and Store Locator traffic quality before scaling spend.
- Use landing page review to separate media problems from page-experience problems.
- Keep reporting simple: weekly trend, channel split, device split and landing page action tracker.

## Boundary

This report uses simulated portfolio data only and does not claim access to real advertising, client, user or analytics data.
