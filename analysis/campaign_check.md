# Six campaigns that never happened

Real orders: 36,969 from 5,878 accounts of a UK wholesaler (UCI *Online Retail II*). No campaign is in the data, so the right answer is: no effect.

Every 60 days for a year, the accounts that were late against their own ordering rhythm were "contacted" (nothing was sent), one in 5 was left out, and the orders of the next 60 days were counted.

| Start | Contacted | Ordered | Share | Their orders | Left out | Ordered | Share | Difference, points (margin) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 2010-12-01 | 156 | 55 | 35% | GBP 126,336 | 39 | 15 | 38% | -3 (-20 to +12) |
| 2011-01-30 | 364 | 140 | 38% | GBP 98,601 | 91 | 43 | 47% | -9 (-20 to +2) |
| 2011-03-31 | 440 | 155 | 35% | GBP 110,190 | 110 | 46 | 42% | -7 (-17 to +3) |
| 2011-05-30 | 472 | 141 | 30% | GBP 83,931 | 118 | 40 | 34% | -4 (-14 to +5) |
| 2011-07-29 | 488 | 152 | 31% | GBP 103,436 | 122 | 33 | 27% | +4 (-5 to +12) |
| 2011-09-27 | 480 | 217 | 45% | GBP 199,004 | 120 | 47 | 39% | +6 (-4 to +15) |
| **All six** | 2,400 | 860 | 36% | GBP 721,498 | 600 | 224 | 37% | -2 (-6 to +3) |

**What a campaign report would have claimed:** 860 times an account came back, GBP 721,498 of orders. **What the left-out accounts say:** 37% of them ordered too, against 36%. The campaigns did nothing, because there were none.

## How often the check is fooled

300 made-up campaign names for each of the six dates, so 1,800 different draws.

| Left out | Campaigns | Called "worked" | Called "hurt" |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 in 5 | 1,800 | 38 (2.1%) | 42 (2.3%) |
| 1 in 3 | 1,800 | 53 (2.9%) | 41 (2.3%) |
| 1 in 2 | 1,800 | 40 (2.2%) | 53 (2.9%) |

With a 95% margin, about 2.5% on each side is expected.

## How big an effect it can see

An effect is added by hand: some contacted accounts that did not order are counted as if they had. Share of campaigns in which the check says "worked":

| Left out | +10 points | +15 points | +20 points |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 in 5 | 45% | 77% | 93% |
| 1 in 3 | 56% | 87% | 97% |
| 1 in 2 | 64% | 90% | 98% |

On lists of 200 to 700 accounts, a small effect stays invisible. The plan says so before the campaign starts.
