Which customers deserve which CRM action, and where should media budget move?

RFM segmentation, cohort retention and lifetime value on a real UK online retailer, followed by attribution, an A/B test and a budget model on simulated campaign data. Every card says which kind of data it uses.

What the numbers say

  1. Protect Champions before buying reach: 26% of customers, 71% of revenue (VIP & referral flow).
  2. Judge acquisition cost by market: a customer in Germany is worth 48% more over their lifetime than one in United Kingdom.
  3. Do not fund media on last click: it under-credits Display by 266 conversions and over-credits Paid Search by 363 Simulated
