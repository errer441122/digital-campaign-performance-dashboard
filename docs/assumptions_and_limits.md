# Assumptions And Limits

## Assumptions

- The dataset represents a six-week simulated campaign sample.
- Campaigns are simplified into four channels: Paid Search, Paid Social, Email and Display.
- Landing page performance is simulated from click/session behavior and does not come from a real analytics platform.
- Revenue attribution is simplified and uses last-touch style logic for portfolio readability.
- Device, audience and landing page labels are intentionally generic.

## Limits

- No real company, client, user, advertising, CRM or analytics data is used.
- The workbook is a portfolio artifact, not a production dashboard.
- The dataset does not include creative-level performance, keyword detail, attribution windows, offline conversions, consent mode effects or incrementality testing.
- Recommendations are practical examples based on the simulated pattern, not advice for a real account.

## Responsible positioning

This project is designed to show a junior analytics workflow: KPI definition, data organization, dashboarding, insight writing and documented limitations.
