# KPI Dictionary

| KPI | Formula | How to read it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Impressions | Platform-reported ad views | Reach / delivery volume. |
| Clicks | Platform-reported clicks | Traffic generated by campaign activity. |
| CTR | `clicks / impressions` | Engagement rate. Higher is usually better when traffic quality is stable. |
| CPC | `cost_eur / clicks` | Cost paid for each click. Lower is better if conversion quality is stable. |
| Conversions | Simulated target actions | Main business action, such as lead, booking or purchase. |
| Conversion rate | `conversions / clicks` | Share of visitors who completed the target action. |
| CPA | `cost_eur / conversions` | Cost per conversion. Lower is better when conversion value is comparable. |
| Revenue | Simulated revenue attributed to conversions | Business value generated by campaign activity. |
| ROAS | `revenue_eur / cost_eur` | Return on ad spend. Higher is better. |
| Landing page performance | Sessions, bounce rate, duration, conversion rate | Helps explain whether traffic quality or page experience is limiting results. |
| Weekly trend | KPI movement by campaign week | Shows whether performance is improving, stable or declining over time. |

## Notes

- Percent fields are stored as decimal rates in the CSV files.
- Euro fields are simulated and should be read as portfolio examples, not real commercial data.
- KPI interpretation should combine efficiency and scale. A low CPA channel may still need budget limits if volume is small.
