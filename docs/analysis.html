What I would do with this in a real shop

These are recommendations, not results. The dataset has no email or advertising data, so nothing here has been tested on customers.

Send the second-order email before day 71

Half of the new customers who come back within a year place their second order within 71 days, and by day 90, 41% of new customers have already ordered again. A reminder without a discount fits before day 71; the discount is better kept for the ones still missing after day 90.

Start the win-back at day 91, not after a year

Customers who stopped buying three to six months ago are a short list with a recent purchase history. The group silent for more than a year is 2.7 times larger, and the longer the silence, the harder the return.

Keep discounts for the customers who need a reason to return

Send the offer to the win-back group only. The best customers get early access or a thank-you instead, and long-inactive, low-value customers get one cheap reminder before they leave the mailing list.

Judge the cost of a new customer by market

If a customer in Germany spends 48% more over time, paying more to acquire one there can still make sense. One blended target for every country hides that.
