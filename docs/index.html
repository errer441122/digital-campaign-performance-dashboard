What it cannot do

  • See small effects on short lists.With 500 accounts it recognises an effect of about 15 points. If you expect 3, do not run the test: the plan tells you before you start.
  • Work if the left-out accounts are contacted anyway.The sales team has to agree to the list. The biggest accounts can be kept out of the test and contacted always.
  • Judge revenue.It shows it, but one large order moves the total, so the verdict counts accounts that ordered.
  • Prove it on a company's own campaign, yet.It has been tested on public data and on files written by hand. The first real campaign is still to come.
